## SEO Technical Setup
- **Meta titles**: 50-60 chars, include primary keyword
- **Meta descriptions**: 150-160 chars, include CTA
- **URL structure**: `/blog/best-underwear-for-leggings`
- **Image alt text**: Descriptive with keywords
- **Internal linking**: Every blog post links to 2+ product pages
- **Schema markup**: Product, Article, BreadcrumbList
