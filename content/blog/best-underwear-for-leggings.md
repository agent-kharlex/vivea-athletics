## What Makes Underwear Good for Leggings?

Before we get to the recommendations, here's what to look for:

1. **Seamless or flat seams** — No visible panty lines (VPL)
2. **Cameltoe-proof design** — A gusset or spacer panel that prevents fabric from riding up
3. **Moisture-wicking fabric** — Keeps you dry during workouts
4. **Stay-put waistband** — No rolling or slipping during movement
5. **Breathable material** — Prevents bacteria and odor

## How to Prevent Camel Toe in Leggings: Quick Tips

Even with the right underwear, here are additional tips:

- **Size up in leggings** if you're between sizes — tight fabric is the #1 cause
- **Choose thick, opaque leggings** — thin fabric shows everything
- **Look for leggings with a gusset** — the diamond-shaped panel prevents riding
- **Avoid front seams** — seamless leggings are your friend
- **Try a thong style** — less fabric means less risk of camel toe

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What underwear doesn't show through leggings?
Seamless thongs or cameltoe-proof underwear with a 3D spacer panel are best. Look for "no-show" or "VPL-free" labels.

### Should you wear underwear under leggings?
It's personal preference. Many women prefer underwear for hygiene and comfort. The right underwear (like Vivea) is invisible under leggings.

### What's the difference between a thong and a bodysuit for leggings?
Thongs cover less but are cooler and less visible. Bodysuits offer full coverage and support, great for high-impact activities.

### Can I wear regular cotton underwear with leggings?
You can, but cotton holds moisture and can show lines. Synthetic moisture-wicking fabrics are better for workouts.
