# How to Prevent Camel Toe in Leggings: 7 Proven Tips

*Last updated: August 2026*

Camel toe — or "crotch cleavage" as Gen Z calls it — is the #1 wardrobe malfunction women face when wearing leggings. Whether you're at the gym, running errands, or in a yoga class, it's uncomfortable and embarrassing. The good news? It's completely preventable.

## Why Does Camel Toe Happen?

Camel toe occurs when tight fabric (like leggings) presses against the crotch area, creating a visible outline. It's caused by:

- **Tight leggings** — Fabric that's too small pulls up
- **Thin fabric** — Sheer material shows everything
- **Front seams** — A seam right in the center creates a ridge
- **Wrong underwear** — Regular underwear bunches and rides up
- **No gusset** — Leggings without a gusset (the diamond-shaped panel) are more prone

### 2. Size Up in Leggings

If your leggings are too tight, they'll ride up. If you're between sizes, always size up. The fabric should be snug but not skin-tight. You should be able to pinch a small amount of fabric at the thigh.

### 3. Choose Leggings with a Gusset

A gusset is a diamond-shaped panel sewn into the crotch area. It reduces tension and prevents the fabric from riding up. Most quality leggings have this — check the product description before buying.

### 4. Avoid Front Seams

Seamless leggings or leggings with a flat front panel are best. A front seam creates a ridge that can show through, especially in lighter colors.

### 5. Opt for Thicker, Opaque Fabric

Thin leggings show everything. Look for "squat-proof" or "opaque" labels. If you can see your hand through the fabric when stretched, it's too thin.

### 6. Try a Thong Style

Thongs are naturally cameltoe-resistant because there's less fabric in the front to ride up. A cameltoe-proof thong is the ultimate combination — minimal fabric plus a protective panel.

### 7. Use a Camel Toe Guard (Temporary Fix)

If you don't have cameltoe-proof underwear, silicone camel toe guards stick to your skin and create a smooth surface. They're a temporary solution but work in a pinch.
